### The Foundation of Justin Timberlake's Fame

Justin Timberlake's widespread fame originated from his role as the lead singer of the iconic boy band *NSYNC* in the late 1990s. The band achieved phenomenal global success with numerous chart-topping hits. Following *NSYNC*'s hiatus, Timberlake launched a highly successful solo career with his debut album *Justified* (2002), featuring hit singles like "Cry Me a River" and "Rock Your Body." His subsequent albums, such as *FutureSex/LoveSounds* (2006) with tracks like "SexyBack" and "What Goes Around... Comes Around," solidified his position as a global music superstar. Furthermore, Timberlake has expanded his career into acting, with notable roles in films like *The Social Network* and voice work in the *Trolls* movie franchise, showcasing his versatility and maintaining his long-term relevance.
